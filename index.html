Why notice deadlines matter more than estimating

Up to 20% of construction profit gets lost to unbilled extras — work that happened but was never formally noticed, documented, or billed in time. Most GC agreements set a strict window (often 14–21 calendar days) to submit written notice after a change or claim event. Miss it, and you can lose the right to be paid for that work entirely, regardless of how good your original estimate was.
